Research Questions

RQ1: What physical-world information becomes visible through egocentric perception that remains invisible through digital traces alone?
RQ2: In what contexts does this information improve personalization vs. introduce noise?
RQ3: What design requirements emerge for integrating continuous sensing into agent memory?

Approach

Exploratory + Autoethnographic

Not assuming — Discovering

  • But: Discovering where it helps and where it doesn't

Autoethnographic Self-Study

  • Design Science Research (artifact + empirical findings)

Why Egocentric Vision?

  1. Mobility — Moves with you, continuous capture across locations
    Consumer smart glasses with cameras are going viral
  2. Information density — Environment, activity, objects in one channel
  3. Implicit attention signal — What appears = what you engage with
  4. Technical maturity — VLMs can convert video to structured text

Auxiliary sensors: audio, IMU

Why OpenClaw?

  1. Deployed baseline
    One of few open-source personal AI agents in sustained real-world use
  2. Integration opportunity
    Personalizes through a workspace of plain markdown files
  3. Inspectability
    Human-readable format, easy to debug and extend

System Design: Three Layers

Layer 1: Capture (continuous)
Egocentric capture → Privacy filtering → Trigger/tags

↓

Layer 1.5: Pre-Processing (passive)
Discard junk → Importance scoring → Batch boundary

↓

Layer 2: Inference (batch)
VLM API call (video → text) → Observation + annotation

↓

Layer 3: Memory Integration
Logs (daily) → Insights (monthly) → Patterns (long-term)
